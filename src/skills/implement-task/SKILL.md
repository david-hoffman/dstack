# Implement task

Package version: [VERSION](../../VERSION). Release policy: [package versioning](../../README.md#package-versioning).

Use one recorded mode and the approved task's pinned local policy throughout. Choose the route before model/effort and execution, following [specification section 4](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#4-risk-scaled-delivery-sessions).

| Approved risk route | Mode, ownership, and acceptance |
|---|---|
| Mechanical or documentation | Worker edits non-normative prose, formatting, links, or deterministic changes with a reviewable diff. No changed executable behavior, dependency, verification rule, permission, or policy. Review the diff and relevant deterministic checks; owner acceptance is sufficient unless the contract requires independent review. |
| Bounded existing behavior or infrastructure maintenance | A small conventional repair/refactor or scoped dependency/lint/workflow maintenance preserving settled expectations and platform/check semantics. Worker may inspect implementation and edit explicitly authorized product code, tests, fixtures, and maintenance infrastructure. A fresh independent bounded reviewer checks the oracle, product and infrastructure changes, preserved gates, scope, and exact passing candidate; the worker cannot approve their repair. |
| High risk | Scientific/numerical behavior, binary formats, new custom oracles, security/permission or execution-authority boundaries, substantial interfaces, unresolved semantics, or uncertain impact require fresh blind root A/B, a fresh root implementation author, and fresh D. Product work uses restricted C. Infrastructure-only work uses an explicitly authorized infrastructure author for precisely named infrastructure/settings. Mixed work records separate fresh product C and infrastructure-author ownership; A/B/D cover all approved behavior and authority. |

Risk follows meaning and impact, not changed-line count. Uncertainty promotes the task. A mixed task takes the highest tier unless independently scoped contracts justify a split. Removing tests/assertions requires a reviewed obligation mapping; unchanged runtime alone does not preserve proof. Changed tests/fixtures always require independent review, using blind A/B when high risk. Dependency, workflow, and gate maintenance always needs independent review; changed execution authority/security boundaries use high risk. Coverage, discovery, fixtures, and delivery-rule changes each need their own authorized scope. No route permits weakening checks.

Policy changes use a separate, explicitly owner-approved documentation/doctor path and fresh independent policy review before activation. They cannot classify themselves as mechanical and do not require fabricated product-red evidence or scientific A/B tests. Do not activate proposed rules or replace an in-flight task's pinned policy without explicit owner-approved migration; preserve earlier attempts and allowances.

## Worker — mechanical or bounded

Confirm the actual owner instruction, recorded scope/tier, checks, budget, gate mode, per-role model/effort, escalation triggers, and remaining allowances. For these two routes, a complete explicit owner instruction may authorize the task; record it and send the concise read-back without demanding a second approval solely because the record followed the instruction. Silence never supplies approval. Missing material decisions block dependent work.

Make the smallest authorized change. Prefer existing code, dependencies, and conventions. In bounded mode, own the named product, test, fixture, and maintenance files together; do not borrow C's restricted packet and assume it authorizes these edits. Derive or challenge test expectations against the approved public contract, not the current output. Preserve accepted behavior and all check semantics. A failed lower-tier check does not authorize a scientific, interface, permission, or policy repair. On a promotion trigger, stop dependent work and return to intake. The owner must explicitly approve the identified high-risk contract, route, precisely named infrastructure/settings and product ownership, checks, and budget before high-risk work begins. A bounded worker cannot self-promote, switch modes to continue, or relax C's restrictions.

Use regression/public-behavior evidence appropriate to the task; do not manufacture a red test for a refactor or a prose edit. Return the exact candidate and evidence for fresh bounded review whenever required, including changed tests/fixtures or infrastructure. A correction needs renewed independent review; do not fix and approve your own repair.

## C — high-risk implementer

Start initial C as a fresh **root session** with the approved task, pinned permitted policy, relevant project docs, reviewed tests, and recorded Git test checkpoint. A subagent or a fork inheriting another role's conversation is not a fresh root. Do not inherit A's conversation.

1. Confirm explicit approval of the identified high-risk contract, scope, B acceptance, checkpoint, and meaningful baseline red or appropriate regression evidence. Missing evidence means stop, not fill it in yourself.
2. Make the smallest product change. Avoid speculative abstractions, unrequested refactors, and new services.
3. **Do not change tests, fixtures, snapshots, discovery/coverage configuration, workflows, skills, AGENTS.md, or the delivery spec.** This is a prompt rule; a failing check grants no exception.
4. A suspected test defect, missing requirement, or infrastructure issue returns to the appropriate test/intake/setup owner. The coordinator gives blind A/B only public-contract reproductions and permitted correction inputs, never C's implementation, conversation, coverage-line maps, or copied implementation-bearing reports. Tests added after implementation are supplemental.
5. C may continue its own session for an authorized in-scope repair. It gains no new file scope, budget, or repair allowance. All D correction verdicts use fresh independent root review without C's conversation.

## Infrastructure author — explicitly authorized high risk

For an infrastructure-only high-risk task, use fresh blind root A → fresh blind root B → fresh **root infrastructure author** → fresh D. This author fills the infrastructure implementation slot separately from product C; it is not an exception to C's absolute restricted-file rules. For mixed scope, record separate fresh product C and infrastructure-author sessions with precisely named responsibilities; A/B tests and D review cover the complete approved behavior and authority change.

Start only with explicit owner approval of the identified high-risk contract/route, named infrastructure files or settings, checks, budget, pinned policy, B-accepted tests, recorded test checkpoint, and appropriate baseline/test-first evidence. Do not inherit another author's conversation. A subagent, reused bounded-worker session, or inherited fork is not a fresh root. Missing or ambiguous ownership stops dependent work.

Edit **only the precisely named infrastructure/settings**. A/B own authorized tests, fixtures, and snapshots; product C owns product changes. Do not edit those other owners' files or delivery-policy instructions. Discovery/coverage or other gate infrastructure requires its own expressly named authorization and preserved verification obligations; authority changes never permit weakening accepted checks or self-approval. Missing tests or semantics return through blind A/B or intake using public-contract reproductions, never raw author conversation or implementation-bearing diagnostics. Documentation policy amendments use the separate owner-approved documentation/policy-review path.

Return the exact infrastructure/settings candidate and evidence for fresh D, following the full local and required CI gates below. An authorized infrastructure repair requires a **fresh root infrastructure author and fresh D**, within the same task-wide repair allowance; there is no infrastructure-author continuation exception. D never inherits either this author's or product C's conversation. Select the recorded model/effort for execution-authority, implementation, and tool complexity rather than diff size.

## Checks and evidence — every mode

Run cheap bounded prerequisites before expensive dependent checks: actual interpreter/platform, dependency-lock identity, installation/import health, required isolated/nested environment creation, and actual reviewer launch/authentication/connectivity when review is required. A known environment path or configured reviewer name is not proof of current health. Stop dependent phases on a prerequisite failure, retain applicable independent results, and diagnose before retrying. Probe host wait/notification support and connectivity; use bounded waits with timeout/failure handling and yield during unchanged status. Unsupported host setup needs separate authorization; no daemon, scheduler, or automatic repair loop.

The default submission gate is canonical **full local verification of the exact candidate before opening/updating a PR**. Readiness additionally requires full CI on every required platform and independent review where the route requires it. Incremental checks guide edits; they do not replace these gates. Keep 100% measured statement and branch coverage across instrumentable owned runtime, globally and per package, including never-imported files and applicable subprocesses. Disclose exclusions and unsupported measurement. Require every shard and complete reports for each platform/package; never union platforms to conceal a gap. Profile slow tests before restructuring; helper/shard changes need their own authorized infrastructure task and independent review, without deleting slow tests or weakening independent clean-state/negative-control claims.

A compact evidence receipt records tested revision/tree, test revision, canonical commands, lock identity, relevant tools/environment/platform, integration base, exact result/coverage, and unresolved limits. Fingerprint material ignored/untracked inputs separately. Keep final candidate identity/results outside its tracked tree, linking full output from ordinary ignored artifacts when useful. For Git-derived versions, include commit/tag/dirty state, build configuration, and resolved artifact/version; identical source trees at different head/test-merge/merge identities need not produce the same artifact. Reuse only evidence whose relevant inputs are unchanged and not challenged by a new failure; environment changes invalidate affected results. Immutable builds are reusable only when all material build/version inputs match.

An optional CI-authoritative submission mode requires a **separately approved policy pilot**, recorded task eligibility, and read-only verification of effective native target-branch protections/rulesets, trusted check sources, current-base/integration requirements, and bypass settings. It is limited to the first two routes. High risk, diagnosis, uncertain evidence, and edits to the pilot's own workflows, discovery/coverage, protection, build/release proof, or authority retain full local gating. Unknown/ineffective controls or unavailable exact integration/artifact proof block eligibility; configure controls only through separately authorized setup.

In an eligible pilot, pass the approved focused public-behavior/inexpensive local gate before submission or repair update; full canonical CI on every required platform and fresh required review must pass for the exact final candidate before readiness and an owner-authorized merge. The native aggregate must fail for any failed, cancelled, skipped, neutral, absent, or incomplete platform/shard/report. Record head, base/integration, environment, artifact/version, and complete coverage; renew invalidated evidence/review when inputs change. A PR awaiting full CI is unverified. Known applicable failures block submission until classified repair and relevant local checks establish their cause is addressed. A failure discovered in full CI stops readiness, consumes existing allowances, and needs renewed focused local checks/full CI/review. The pilot permits an unexecuted full local suite, never a known failing candidate or missing proof. Revert to default gating when pilot prerequisites lapse.

Never send D or the bounded final reviewer a known failing or incomplete candidate. Keep failed attempts and classify them; do not retry until green without diagnosis. Renew owner approval for changed behavior/interfaces/permissions/data sharing, tier downgrade, budget extension, or exhausted repair allowance. Routine approved transitions, in-scope checks/repairs within allowance, and approved unchanged-scenario redistribution need no repeat permission.

Select C's model/effort for implementation/tool complexity, normally high effort for numerical, binary, concurrency, or resource reasoning; select infrastructure-author effort for execution-authority and implementation/tool complexity. Select bounded worker and reviewer separately and calibrate mechanical work for its deterministic demands. Record supported actual configurations and applicable calibration. No named-model ranking, automatic fallback, or model change that clears exposure/attempts is implied. Charge calibration, failed launches, escalation, and rework to the budget.

At most **one implementation repair task-wide by default**, shared across workers, product C, and infrastructure authors. Separate authors or modes do not create separate allowances. Preserve spending, review windows/rounds, and repairs across continuations, narrower corrections, model swaps, renamed tasks, and migrations. Stop at budget/allowance exhaustion. Return one Current state and concise handoff: decision, exact revision, changed paths/classified findings, evidence links, next role, and remaining allowances. Worker readiness, an independent verdict, native checks, and the owner's explicit merge/release action are separate. Append only useful evidence-backed entries to target root `LESSONS/`; no transcripts or private reasoning. Zero entries is valid.
