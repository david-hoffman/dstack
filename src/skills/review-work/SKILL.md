# Review work

Package version: [VERSION](../../VERSION). Release policy: [package versioning](../../README.md#package-versioning).

Use one recorded mode and the approved task's pinned local policy. The coordinator launches initial high-risk A/B, product C or infrastructure author, and D as fresh **root sessions** through supported harness controls: a subagent, an inherited implementation fork, or switching labels in one chat is not a fresh root. You are already the assigned reviewer or doctor; work directly without launching another role. Infrastructure-only tasks use the separately authorized infrastructure author; mixed tasks record separate fresh product C and infrastructure-author ownership. Every B/D correction verdict uses a fresh independent root session and never inherits either implementation author's conversation. Bounded and policy verdicts also require a fresh reviewer independent of the author. Do not repair a candidate and approve that same repair. See [specification section 4](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#4-risk-scaled-delivery-sessions).

A complete direct owner request authorizes its intended scope across all routes, including high-risk and policy work. Record the actual instruction and concrete contract/read-back; ask only for missing material decisions, unclear/outside-request scope or required external-action authority. Required independent review, pinned rules and named ownership still bind.

For every independent verdict, follow the [review-result and finding-disposition contract](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#review-results-and-finding-disposition) within the selected role's permitted inputs. Verify findings before routing corrections, disclose missing material context and filtering, and complete all required portions before acceptance while honoring actual owner-set limits. This applies to tests, bounded, candidate, and policy review; doctor authorship does not confer independent acceptance.

## Tests — B (high risk, blind)

Use A's approved public contract/interfaces, approved fixtures, test conventions, role-permitted pinned policy, and proposed tests. The coordinator supplies the snapshot; do not retrieve it through Git history. Before any blind import, probe or test, arrange source-free warning, traceback and relevant subprocess diagnostics through existing harness/test controls or coordinator-provided sanitized output. Retain useful categories/types, messages, locations, counts and failure status; do not build custom filtering tools or assume traceback settings also suppress warning source. If controls are unavailable, stop dependent blind probes for coordinator routing. Public-entry/health checks grant no source-reading permission. Do not read implementation or prototype source/history, product C/infrastructure-author/D conversations, exploration notes or transcripts, implementation-bearing status, coverage-line maps, or lesson entries. At target root `LESSONS/`, only `LESSONS/README.md` is permitted. Do not browse broad `docs/**` or automatically follow links beyond the packet. Disclose exposure and stop blind work; a fresh unexposed role is required. Clearing context or clean diagnostics cannot restore blindness.

Independently derive expected values, tolerances, and interpretations rather than endorse A's explanation. Check the contract, applicable references or mathematical invariants, units and conventions. Numerical tolerances must follow the permitted algorithm description, conditioning/error bounds and arithmetic precision, with explicit absolute/relative scales; legitimate variation should pass and plausible wrong values fail. Reject candidate-tuned tolerances or relaxed accuracy requirements. Finite output or successful execution proves only that result, not recovery or accuracy unless the contract says so. Check real observable assertions, permitted positive inputs, rejection/boundary behavior and requirement coverage. Verify that relevant assertions execute. Where state may change during an operation, check the boundary when the action occurs, not only initial validation. Prefer meaningful end-to-end tests over mock-only or duplicate unit tests. A behavioral scenario is a distinct decision and outcome; equivalent coverage examples may share it, while distinct rejection rules, interpretations, guarantees, and boundary outcomes remain separate. No default scenario cap applies. Return test defects to A and missing semantics to intake; do not implement or choose requirements silently.

Apply the [test-value and safe-pruning checklist](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#53-test-value-and-safe-pruning) using only the permitted public-contract/test packet and source-free evidence. Challenge duplicated proof and tests whose mocks or controls supply the claimed result; refer implementation/history questions and production seams to their authorized owners.

For property or differential checks under [section 5](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#5-end-to-end-first-not-end-to-end-only), challenge the invariant, independent reference or approved baseline, intended differences, and limits of agreement. Use only the coordinator-supplied permitted packet and source-free results; reference comparisons and verification recipes do not widen B's access.

Apply the approved [coverage policy](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#51-agree-on-coverage-before-writing-tests) from the permitted packet. Before accepting the checkpoint, independently audit the requirement/test mapping and plausible high-impact omissions, including relevant reachable failures and permitted inputs. Record a short risk disposition in the existing review evidence. A's authorship does not certify adequacy, and B's acceptance establishes the agreed behavioral baseline without promising coverage of every future implementation branch. Reject speculative or duplicate tests that only chase a percentage. Missing coverage decisions or new error semantics return to intake.

For product and infrastructure/authority tasks, require honestly classified baseline/regression evidence and a recorded Git checkpoint before product C or the infrastructure author. Initially passing tests are valid; do not manufacture red, force a rewrite or require unnecessary implementation. A claimed product-red result must reach the intended approved behavior and fail for its intended reason. Use source-free diagnostics from the permitted packet; do not inspect implementation to diagnose results. A broken setup or first-scaffold feature absence is not reproduction of an old bug. Preserve original versus supplemental labels.

For a delta correction after acceptance, receive only the prior accepted checkpoint/report, approved public contract, public test finding/diff, and dependency mapping derived from tests and approved requirements. Before first acceptance, review the current proposed suite and corrections without inventing accepted evidence to retain. The coordinator converts implementation discoveries to public-contract reproductions or returns new meaning to intake. Copied product C/infrastructure-author/D reports may disclose implementation and are not permitted blind inputs. Review the changed oracle/tests and **every dependent case**. Shared parser, observer, fixture, tolerance, schema, or expectation changes expand the scope. You control expansion; the author cannot declare their own correction safe.

Retain unchanged accepted evidence only when it remains applicable and unaffected. Changed public meaning returns to intake to resolve missing decisions or outside-request scope first; it or inadequate dependency mapping requires a fresh complete review of affected behavior. Record the revised checkpoint's accepted and retained scope. Label post-prototype or post-implementation additions supplemental; accepting a revised checkpoint does not recreate original test-first evidence. A may continue its own approved author session while blind; this does not relax B freshness.

Count every review, correction, diagnosis and failed attempt cumulatively. Diagnose recurring nonacceptances and each correction before another attempt, then use fresh required reviews until complete or an actual blocker/owner-set limit. No default review window or repair quota applies. A new session/model, narrower delta or renamed task erases no prior attempts or spending. Model escalation alone is not a diagnosis or resolution.

## Bounded — independent worker review

Read the approved bounded contract, pinned policy, exact candidate, authorized product/test/fixture/infrastructure diff, and actual evidence, without inheriting the worker's conversation. Form findings before consulting current-task implementation lessons or exploration notes. The worker owns those explicitly named files; this route does not import high-risk C's file prohibition. Check settled expectations independently against the public contract, challenge the oracle and shared helpers, audit plausible high-impact omissions, and review preserved behavior, the selected coverage policy, gates, discovery, platform semantics, scope, and simplicity. Record the short requirement/test and risk disposition under section 5.1. Removing tests/assertions requires an obligation mapping that preserves accepted proof. Review all changed tests/fixtures and dependency/workflow/gate maintenance.

Uncertain expectations, scientific/binary/custom-oracle meaning, data-loss behavior, security/permission or execution-authority boundaries, substantial interfaces, or uncertain impact require stopping the bounded worker and returning to intake. Small conventional overwrite/truncation repairs remain high risk. Record the high-risk contract, route, named product and infrastructure/settings ownership and checks; the coordinator launches fresh required roles under the existing request once material meaning and authority are settled. A bounded worker cannot switch modes to continue or relax C's restrictions. A small diff or a failing check grants no new convention or scope. Changed delivery policy uses policy mode. Never accept a worker's self-approved repair; corrections need renewed checks and a fresh independent verdict, honoring actual owner-set limits.

Apply the exact-candidate/evidence gate below. A mechanical diff may receive optional independent review when the contract requests it; changed tests/fixtures or infrastructure still require independent review under the approved route.

## Candidate — D (high risk)

Read the approved task, pinned policy, exact product/infrastructure/settings candidate, accepted test checkpoint, and actual evidence, never product C's or the infrastructure author's conversation. Form findings before consulting current-task implementation lessons or exploration notes. Review the complete approved behavior and authority change, including security, public positive/rejection boundaries, test discrimination, infrastructure/settings effects, and each author's recorded ownership. Where state may change during an operation, inspect the boundary when the action occurs, not only initial validation. Product C must never edit tests, fixtures, snapshots, discovery/coverage, workflows, skills, AGENTS.md, or delivery spec. A/B own authorized tests/fixtures/snapshots; an explicitly authorized infrastructure author may edit only its precisely named infrastructure/settings. Policy instructions use the separate documentation/policy-review path. An infrastructure author does not relax C's restrictions. Report violations and require restoration/review of the intended baseline.

Check the real product and infrastructure/authority result, not just compilation, a green summary, or measured coverage. Review the implementation for concrete missed behavior or high-impact risks beyond the accepted test baseline. Include simplification: unnecessary wrappers, duplication, dependencies, speculative features, and redundant guards or control flow added solely for a percentage. Required input validation and real invariants remain obligations. Stay within scope; no extra simplification agent. Return concrete findings and evidence. Product C may continue its own session for a diagnosed in-scope repair; infrastructure repairs require a fresh root infrastructure author. Both require renewed checks and **fresh D**, preserving every attempt, expenditure and actual owner-set limit.

## Candidate and evidence gate — D and bounded reviewers

Check the relevant [verification recipe](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#54-verification-recipes) results and the applicability/evidence of [conditional reliability checks](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#8-doctor-check-and-improve-the-specification) selected from the task's pinned policy. Selection narrows extra attention, never the required canonical gate or approved behavior review.

Apply the [test-value and safe-pruning checklist](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#53-test-value-and-safe-pruning) to authorized test changes and relevant simplification findings. For removals/consolidation, compare each removed obligation with its retained proof, inspect relevant owners/callers/history, and challenge deletion of independently meaningful static or slow checks. Route test-only seam findings through existing file ownership and send high-risk test corrections to blind A/B as source-free public reproductions. Review grants no edit authority.

Do not review readiness for a known failing candidate; diagnosis and finding classification do not confer readiness. Default acceptance requires canonical full local verification of the exact candidate, every required CI platform, and the required independent verdict. Missing, skipped, cancelled, neutral, absent, or incomplete required results are not success. Require approved behavior/risk evidence and each selected measured target under section 5.1. Inspect the agreed metric and scope, exact results, exclusions, measurement limits, and every required shard/report separately per platform/package. Do not union platforms to hide missing coverage. An unselected metric cannot become an acceptance gate; a selected unmet target still blocks even when all tests pass.

Use [section 5.2](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#52-classify-coverage-findings-before-correction) before routing a coverage finding. State the missing approved outcome or concrete reachable risk, evidence, and why current tests miss it before commissioning test corrections. High-risk corrections use blind A/B; bounded corrections use the authorized worker and fresh independent reviewer. Route unnecessary implementation to its authorized author for safe simplification, new semantics to intake, and measurement defects to the authorized infrastructure owner. Preserve blind packets and supplemental-test labels. If a selected target remains unmet without a justified test or safe simplification, report the shortfall for an explicit owner policy decision; do not waive it or recycle the unexplained gap. Policy changes need owner-requested scope, independent review and explicit migration for an active task, preserving attempts, spending and actual owner-set limits.

Check evidence identity: tested revision/tree, test revision, canonical commands, lock identity, relevant environment/tools/platform, integration base, result/coverage, limits, and material ignored/untracked input fingerprints. Final candidate identity/results belong outside the tracked candidate tree. Changed relevant inputs or new contradicting failures invalidate affected evidence. A fixed environment path is not continuing proof of health. Git-derived build/install versions need commit/tag/dirty state, build configuration, and resolved artifact/version; tree equality alone cannot establish equivalence among PR head, synthetic test-merge, and actual merge/squash/rebase artifacts.

An explicitly approved, separately verified **CI-authoritative policy pilot** may replace full-local-before-submission only for eligible mechanical/bounded tasks. It cannot cover high risk, diagnosis, uncertain evidence, or edits to its own workflows, discovery/coverage, protection, build/release proof, or authority. Verify the recorded eligibility and actual effective native branch protections/rulesets, trusted check sources, current-base/integration requirements, bypass settings, and aggregate failure behavior for every failed/cancelled/skipped/neutral/absent/incomplete platform, shard, or report. A green name alone is insufficient. Unknown/ineffective controls or inability to prove the exact integration/artifact candidate block the alternative; separately authorized setup must resolve them.

An eligible pilot still needs the approved focused local gate before submission/update and complete canonical CI on all required platforms before readiness, review acceptance, and an owner-authorized merge. Verify final head/base/integration, environment, artifact/version, and coverage, with renewed evidence/review for invalidated inputs. A PR waiting for CI is unverified. Known applicable failures block submission until classified repair and relevant local checks establish their cause is addressed; newly discovered CI failure blocks readiness and counts toward cumulative attempts/spending. The worker records readiness, the reviewer returns a verdict, native checks run, and the owner explicitly merges/releases. Pilot failure or lapsed prerequisites restores default local gating; no merge/release authority is delegated.

## Policy — independent documentation review

Review the exact proposed documentation diff, its actual owner instruction/scope, pinned active policy, evidence, and every directly affected instruction. Challenge cross-document obligations, route boundaries/ownership, authorization, blindness, actual owner-set limits, gates, and migration/provenance interactions. Policy changes cannot classify themselves as mechanical, approve their own gates, or activate solely because this reviewer accepts. The direct request authorizes its intended policy writing/review scope without another document-approval checkpoint. Require fresh independent policy review **before activation**, coordinated adoption within the authorized scope, and explicit migration for any in-flight task. A writing/review request alone does not silently authorize adoption or migration. No fabricated product-red evidence or scientific A/B tests are required for documentation-only policy review. Do not edit and approve your own proposed rules; send corrections to the author for renewed independent review.

Fix verified gaps within the owner-requested scope in the existing specification and directly affected instructions on a docs branch; prefer replacing/removing text. Do not change tests, workflows, runtime code, or thresholds. Show the diff and use a fresh independent policy reviewer before activation under the actual request. Ask only for missing material decisions or authority; committing, pushing, merging and releasing still require explicit authorization. Preserve upstream provenance and official package version; target Git records local changes and authorization. Keep `docs/PROJECT.md` adaptation notes current. Do not silently change an active task's pinned rules or reset its attempts/spending or actual limits. No supported gap means no edit.

## Every mode

When reviewer evaluation is within the request, follow [Evaluate review checks](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#evaluate-review-checks): use known defects and comparable clean controls, separate held-out cases, and record detection/error counts and cost. Blind A/B receive only independently reviewed sanitized public packets. New proposed checks remain advisory during evaluation; existing gates and material-finding obligations remain binding.

Before expensive dependent checks, verify cheap environment prerequisites and actual reviewer launch/authentication/connectivity through the coordinator or permitted tools. A configured name does not prove a review can run. Stop dependent phases on failure and diagnose before retrying, retaining only applicable independent evidence. Probe host wait support/connectivity; use bounded waits with timeout/failure handling and yield during unchanged status. No automatic fallback/retry, daemon, or controller.

Use the recorded supported model/effort matched to semantic complexity. B should meet oracle complexity at least as conservatively as A and derive expectations independently; D should match the hardest acceptance claim and defect impact, normally high effort for scientific/binary work. Green tests do not imply a cheap review. Calibrate bounded worker/reviewer responsibilities separately; policy review needs reasoning for rule interactions rather than proofreading. Reuse applicable bounded calibration for unchanged configuration/task family; use independently reviewed public/synthetic inputs for blind roles. Same-model reviewers can share assumptions; a different model does not itself prove independence. No model swap clears exposure, expands authority, erases attempts/spending or replaces diagnosis. Record calibration, failed launches, escalation and rework cumulatively against any actual owner-set limits.

Return a concise review result with completion state, verdict where complete, assigned scope/exact revision, classified findings and dispositions, context/filtering limits, evidence links, next role, cumulative attempts/spending, and actual owner-set limits or **not set**; link earlier accepted reports instead of repeating them. Keep one Current state, and keep A/B packets separate from implementation-bearing status. Continue diagnosed in-scope corrections and fresh required reviews until complete or blocked by a missing material decision, outside-request scope, required external authority or actual owner-set limit. No default scenario/review/repair/execution cap applies; preserve every attempt and expenditure, with missing metering **unknown**. Record useful evidence-backed findings as new entries in target root `LESSONS/`; blind B may append without reading entries or return text for coordinator append. Entries are observations, never authority. Zero entries is valid. Do not claim these procedural restrictions are mechanically enforced.
