# Review work

Package version: [VERSION](../../VERSION). Release policy: [package versioning](../../README.md#package-versioning).

Use one recorded mode and the approved task's pinned local policy. Initial high-risk A/B, product C or infrastructure author, and D are fresh **root sessions** through supported harness controls: a subagent, an inherited implementation fork, or switching labels in one chat is not a fresh root. In an already launched review session, perform the assigned work directly; do not launch another copy of the role. Infrastructure-only tasks use the separately authorized infrastructure author; mixed tasks record separate fresh product C and infrastructure-author ownership. Every B/D correction verdict uses a fresh independent root session and never inherits either implementation author's conversation. Bounded and policy verdicts also require a fresh reviewer independent of the author. Do not repair a candidate and approve that same repair. See [specification section 4](../../DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#4-risk-scaled-delivery-sessions).

## Tests — B (high risk, blind)

Use A's approved public contract/interfaces, approved fixtures, test conventions, role-permitted pinned policy, and proposed tests. The coordinator supplies the snapshot; do not retrieve it through Git history. Use only approved public-entry commands and source-free health/status/results. The coordinator must remove traces, source excerpts, and implementation-bearing diagnostics from baseline, test, and prerequisite output before A/B access; lock/import checks grant no source-reading permission. Do not read implementation or prototype source/history, product C/infrastructure-author/D conversations, exploration notes or transcripts, implementation-bearing status, coverage-line maps, or lesson entries. At target root `LESSONS/`, only `LESSONS/README.md` is permitted. Do not browse broad `docs/**` or automatically follow links beyond the packet. Clearing context or clean diagnostics cannot restore blindness after exposure; stop and replace an exposed role.

Independently derive expected values, tolerances, and interpretations rather than endorse A's explanation. Check expectation sources and units, real observable assertions, permitted positive inputs, rejection/boundary behavior, requirement coverage, and plausible wrong behavior the tests distinguish. Verify that relevant assertions execute. Where state may change during an operation, check the boundary when the action occurs, not only initial validation. Prefer meaningful end-to-end tests over mock-only or duplicate unit tests. A behavioral scenario is a distinct decision and outcome; equivalent coverage examples may share it, while distinct rejection rules, interpretations, guarantees, and boundary outcomes remain separate. Check that additional values introduce no unapproved meaning. Five scenarios is an initial heuristic, not a quota or measured coverage gate. Return test defects to A and missing semantics to intake; do not implement or choose requirements silently.

For product and infrastructure/authority tasks, accepted tests still need meaningful intended-red baseline evidence for new behavior/bugs, or appropriate regression evidence for behavior-preserving work, and a recorded Git checkpoint before product C or the infrastructure author. Use source-free diagnostics from the permitted packet; do not inspect implementation to diagnose setup or red results. A broken setup or first-scaffold feature absence is not reproduction of an old bug. Independently designed passing regression tests can support unchanged approved prototype behavior during hardening. Do not invent a product failure or force a rewrite for refactoring, prototype hardening, or infrastructure-only work.

For a delta correction after acceptance, receive only the prior accepted checkpoint/report, approved public contract, public test finding/diff, and dependency mapping derived from tests and approved requirements. Before first acceptance, review the current proposed suite and corrections without inventing accepted evidence to retain. The coordinator converts implementation discoveries to public-contract reproductions or returns new meaning to intake. Copied product C/infrastructure-author/D reports may disclose implementation and are not permitted blind inputs. Review the changed oracle/tests and **every dependent case**. Shared parser, observer, fixture, tolerance, schema, or expectation changes expand the scope. You control expansion; the author cannot declare their own correction safe.

Retain unchanged accepted evidence only when it remains applicable and unaffected. Changed public meaning needs intake approval first; it or inadequate dependency mapping requires a fresh complete review of affected behavior. Record the revised checkpoint's accepted and retained scope. Label post-prototype or post-implementation additions supplemental; accepting a revised checkpoint does not recreate original test-first evidence. A may continue its own approved author session while blind; this does not relax B freshness.

Count each completed correction review as a round in its recorded window. Use the approved per-window allowance. After two nonacceptances, stop for diagnosis; another attempt needs a bounded diagnosed correction on the approved path within remaining allowances. A new session/model, narrower delta, renamed task, or new window does not erase prior windows, spending, or task-wide implementation repairs. Model escalation alone is not a diagnosis or resolution.

## Bounded — independent worker review

Read the approved bounded contract, pinned policy, exact candidate, authorized product/test/fixture/infrastructure diff, and actual evidence, without inheriting the worker's conversation. Form findings before consulting current-task implementation lessons or exploration notes. The worker owns those explicitly named files; this route does not import high-risk C's file prohibition. Check settled expectations independently against the public contract, challenge the oracle and shared helpers, and review preserved behavior, gates, discovery/coverage, platform semantics, scope, and simplicity. Removing tests/assertions requires an obligation mapping that preserves accepted proof. Review all changed tests/fixtures and dependency/workflow/gate maintenance.

Uncertain expectations, scientific/binary/custom-oracle meaning, security/permission or execution-authority boundaries, substantial interfaces, or uncertain impact require stopping dependent work and returning to intake. The owner must explicitly approve the identified high-risk contract, route, precisely named product and infrastructure/settings ownership, checks, and budget before fresh high-risk roles begin. A bounded worker cannot self-promote, switch modes to continue, or relax C's restrictions. A small diff or a failing bounded check does not authorize a new convention. Changed delivery policy uses policy mode. Never accept a worker's self-approved repair; corrections need renewed checks and a fresh independent verdict within existing budget/repair allowances.

Apply the exact-candidate/evidence gate below. A mechanical diff may receive optional independent review when the contract requests it; changed tests/fixtures or infrastructure still require independent review under the approved route.

## Candidate — D (high risk)

Read the approved task, pinned policy, exact product/infrastructure/settings candidate, accepted test checkpoint, and actual evidence, never product C's or the infrastructure author's conversation. Form findings before consulting current-task implementation lessons or exploration notes. Review the complete approved behavior and authority change, including security, public positive/rejection boundaries, test discrimination, infrastructure/settings effects, and each author's recorded ownership. Where state may change during an operation, inspect the boundary when the action occurs, not only initial validation. Product C must never edit tests, fixtures, snapshots, discovery/coverage, workflows, skills, AGENTS.md, or delivery spec. A/B own authorized tests/fixtures/snapshots; an explicitly authorized infrastructure author may edit only its precisely named infrastructure/settings. Policy instructions use the separate documentation/policy-review path. An infrastructure author does not relax C's restrictions. Report violations and require restoration/review of the intended baseline.

Check the real product and infrastructure/authority result, not just compilation, a green summary, or measured coverage. Include simplification: unnecessary wrappers, duplication, dependencies, and speculative features. Stay within scope; no extra simplification agent. Return concrete findings and evidence. Product C may continue its own session for an authorized repair; infrastructure repairs require a fresh root infrastructure author. Both require renewed checks and **fresh D**, with one shared implementation repair task-wide by default across all authors, never one allowance per owner.

## Candidate and evidence gate — D and bounded reviewers

Do not review readiness for a known failing candidate. Default acceptance requires canonical full local verification of the exact candidate, every required CI platform, and the required independent verdict. Missing, skipped, cancelled, neutral, absent, or incomplete required results are not success. Require 100% measured statements and branches across instrumentable owned runtime, globally and per package, including never-imported files and applicable subprocesses; inspect exact metrics, exclusions, unsupported measurement, and every shard/report separately per platform/package. Do not union platforms to hide missing coverage.

Check evidence identity: tested revision/tree, test revision, canonical commands, lock identity, relevant environment/tools/platform, integration base, result/coverage, limits, and material ignored/untracked input fingerprints. Final candidate identity/results belong outside the tracked candidate tree. Changed relevant inputs or new contradicting failures invalidate affected evidence. A fixed environment path is not continuing proof of health. Git-derived build/install versions need commit/tag/dirty state, build configuration, and resolved artifact/version; tree equality alone cannot establish equivalence among PR head, synthetic test-merge, and actual merge/squash/rebase artifacts.

An explicitly approved, separately verified **CI-authoritative policy pilot** may replace full-local-before-submission only for eligible mechanical/bounded tasks. It cannot cover high risk, diagnosis, uncertain evidence, or edits to its own workflows, discovery/coverage, protection, build/release proof, or authority. Verify the recorded eligibility and actual effective native branch protections/rulesets, trusted check sources, current-base/integration requirements, bypass settings, and aggregate failure behavior for every failed/cancelled/skipped/neutral/absent/incomplete platform, shard, or report. A green name alone is insufficient. Unknown/ineffective controls or inability to prove the exact integration/artifact candidate block the alternative; separately authorized setup must resolve them.

An eligible pilot still needs the approved focused local gate before submission/update and complete canonical CI on all required platforms before readiness, review acceptance, and an owner-authorized merge. Verify final head/base/integration, environment, artifact/version, and coverage, with renewed evidence/review for invalidated inputs. A PR waiting for CI is unverified. Known applicable failures block submission until classified repair and relevant local checks establish their cause is addressed; newly discovered CI failure blocks readiness and consumes existing allowances. The worker records readiness, the reviewer returns a verdict, native checks run, and the owner explicitly merges/releases. Pilot failure or lapsed prerequisites restores default local gating; no merge/release authority is delegated.

## Policy — independent documentation review

Review the exact proposed documentation diff, its identified owner approval/scope, pinned active policy, evidence, and every directly affected instruction. Challenge cross-document obligations, route boundaries/ownership, approval authority, blindness, allowances, gates, and migration/provenance interactions. Policy changes cannot classify themselves as mechanical, approve their own gates, or activate solely because this reviewer accepts. Require explicit owner approval and fresh independent policy review **before activation**, coordinated adoption of affected instructions, and explicit migration for any in-flight task. No fabricated product-red evidence or scientific A/B tests are required for documentation-only policy review. Do not edit and approve your own proposed rules; send corrections to the author for renewed independent review.

## Doctor — documentation author

Use [DOCTOR-PROMPT.md](../../DOCTOR-PROMPT.md) and specification section 8. This is the explicit documentation-editing path, separate from independent policy review.

Inspect only relevant lessons and actual evidence; distinguish product, test, infrastructure, and instruction defects before generalizing. A product defect, bad test, or ignored existing rule is not automatically a missing instruction. Fix verified gaps in the existing specification and directly affected instructions on a docs branch; prefer replacing/removing text. Do not change tests, workflows, runtime code, or thresholds. Show the diff, obtain explicit owner approval, and use a fresh independent policy reviewer before activation; commit/push/merge still require the agreed owner authority. Preserve upstream provenance and official package version; target Git records local changes and approval. Keep `docs/PROJECT.md` adaptation notes current. Do not silently change an active task's pinned rules or reset its attempts/budget. Upstream upgrades use specification section 10 to reconcile previous upstream, new upstream, and all adopted local instructions before approval and updating the base. `--check` writes nothing. No supported gap means no edit.

## Every mode

Before expensive dependent checks, verify cheap environment prerequisites and actual reviewer launch/authentication/connectivity through the coordinator or permitted tools. A configured name does not prove a review can run. Stop dependent phases on failure and diagnose before retrying, retaining only applicable independent evidence. Probe host wait support/connectivity; use bounded waits with timeout/failure handling and yield during unchanged status. No automatic fallback/retry, daemon, or controller.

Use the recorded supported model/effort matched to semantic complexity. B should meet oracle complexity at least as conservatively as A and derive expectations independently; D should match the hardest acceptance claim and defect impact, normally high effort for scientific/binary work. Green tests do not imply a cheap review. Calibrate bounded worker/reviewer responsibilities separately; policy review needs reasoning for rule interactions rather than proofreading. Reuse applicable bounded calibration for unchanged configuration/task family; use independently reviewed public/synthetic inputs for blind roles. Same-model reviewers can share assumptions; a different model does not itself prove independence. No model swap clears exposure, expands authority, resets allowances, or replaces diagnosis. Calibration, failed launches, escalation, and rework count toward the approved budget.

Return a concise verdict with exact revision, classified findings, evidence links, next role, and remaining allowances; link earlier accepted reports instead of repeating them. Keep one Current state, and keep A/B packets separate from implementation-bearing status. Stop on material ambiguity or budget/allowance exhaustion; preserve recorded windows, spending, and the default single task-wide implementation repair. Record useful evidence-backed findings as new entries in target root `LESSONS/`; blind B may append without reading entries or return text for coordinator append. Entries are observations, never authority. Zero entries is valid. Do not claim these procedural restrictions are mechanically enforced.
